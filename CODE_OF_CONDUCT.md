# Community conduct

MCP Dashboard welcomes respectful participation from people of every
background and level of experience.

Contributors should discuss ideas and code in good faith, give specific and
constructive feedback, respect privacy, avoid harassment or discrimination,
and never publish another person's credentials, configuration, transcripts, or
private filesystem details. Disagreement about implementation is expected;
personal attacks, threats, intimidation, sexualized conduct, deliberate
disruption, and sustained bad-faith behavior are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other participation
that violates these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate,
private where practical, and focused on protecting the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer. Do not use a public issue
when a report contains sensitive personal or security information.
